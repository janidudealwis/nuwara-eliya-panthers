I — The Club

We built the Panthers around a simple belief: golf rewards patience, precision and respect. So does a great club.

Every detail considered From tee to clubhouse, nothing is left to chance.

Set in the cool highlands of Nuwara Eliya, the club is a place to sharpen your game, compete with purpose and belong to a community that holds itself to a higher standard. No noise, no excess. Only the game, played well.
