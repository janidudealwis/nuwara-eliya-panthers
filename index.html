I — The Team

Golf rewards patience, precision and respect. In the league's first season, the Panthers had all three, and finished on top.

Captain Tharanga Gunasekera Led the Panthers to the inaugural Ceylon Golf League title in December 2025.

The Nuwara Eliya Panthers carry the hill country into the Ceylon Golf League, Sri Lanka's first franchise golf tournament. The league was founded by Mahela Jayawardene, Shamal Perera and Suhayb Sangani. Eight city franchises draft their squads at auction across seven handicap categories, then meet over three days of team match play.

II — Champions 2025

First to the Crown

Royal Colombo Golf Club, 5–7 December 2025. Seven rounds of foursomes, fourball and indies against seven other franchises. The Panthers finished with the most points in the league, six clear of runners-up Trinco Sharks on 18.

League points 24 The highest total of the season
Record 3W · 2T Three wins and two ties across seven rounds
Group stage 105 Points to finish top of Group B
Beyond the trophy

The Panthers gave their entire prize purse to national flood relief.

III — The Format

Three Days, One Crown

Eight city franchises. Squads drafted at auction across seven handicap categories, in men's and women's divisions.

I

Foursomes

Day one. Two players, one ball, alternate shots. A test of partnership under pressure.

II

Fourball

Day two. Two players, each with their own ball. The better score on every hole counts.

III

Indies

Day three. Individual match play, one against three. Every point is earned alone.

IV

The Auction

Every squad is built at the player auction, one handicap category at a time.
